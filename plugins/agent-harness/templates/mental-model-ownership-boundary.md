# Ownership / Boundary Model

- plugin core: target, acceptance, state-sync and containment protocol.
- project adapter: project-specific paths, gates and constraints.
- host: session, resume, workers, terminals, full logs and transient plans.
- artifacts: accepted outcomes and concise verification, not execution history.

## Pause Conditions

- missing product direction or authority;
- credentials, paid calls, production or destructive action;
- conflicting scope or failed required verification.
